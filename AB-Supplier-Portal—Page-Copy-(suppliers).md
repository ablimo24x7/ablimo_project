## How onboarding works

**H2 — From application to first job**

Most suppliers complete onboarding in \[Confirm: N\] working days once all documents are in.

1. **Apply.** Complete the short form below and upload your company documents.
2. **Document review.** Our procurement team checks licences, insurance and registration, and contacts you within \[Confirm: N\] working days.
3. **Vehicle inspection.** We inspect a sample of your vehicles at your depot or an AB location.
4. **Driver briefing.** Your drivers attend a briefing on the AB Chauffeur Standard, our app and dispatch procedures.
5. **Rate agreement.** We agree rates by vehicle class and job type, and sign a supplier agreement.
6. **Trial assignment.** You run a first job with an AB coordinator, then join the approved supplier list and receive work through the portal.

## Day to day

**H2 — What working with AB looks like**

- **Job offers:** You receive job requests with date, vehicle class, route and rate. Accept or decline within the response window.
- **Briefing:** Before each programme, you get the trip sheet, passenger details needed for the pickup and the on-site contact.
- **On the day:** Drivers check in through the AB app. The Riyadh control room monitors trips live and is your single point of contact.
- **Standards:** AB-branded name boards and dress code where the client requires it \[Confirm\]. No subcontracting to a third party without written approval.
- **After the job:** Trip records are confirmed in the portal. You invoice once a month, or per programme for events \[Confirm\], and we pay within \[Confirm: N\] days.
- **Performance review:** Each quarter we share your punctuality, vehicle condition and feedback scores, and agree any improvements.
