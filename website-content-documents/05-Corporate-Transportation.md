## Section 4: How we run it: 5-step managed operation

*Eyebrow Badge:* `OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORK`

When your executives, visitors and staff depend on getting somewhere on time every day, transportation becomes an operation in its own right. AB runs it for you through a rigorous five-stage delivery cycle:

- **1. Requirements review** — Passenger groups, locations, hours, service levels and security needs.
- **2. Programme design** — Routes, vehicle classes, driver numbers, standby cover and booking rules, documented and signed off by you.
- **3. Mobilisation** — Vehicles prepared, drivers briefed on your sites and protocol, booking channels set up.
- **4. Daily operation** — Live tracking, dispatch changes and incident handling from the Riyadh control room, 24/7.
- **5. Monthly review** — Usage, punctuality and cost by department, with recommended changes.

## Section 9: Frequently Asked Questions

*Eyebrow Badge:* `Knowledge Base`

Key commercial and operational answers for facilities managers, travel leads, and HR directors.

### Frequently Asked Questions:

**Q1: What is the difference between a dedicated driver and on-demand chauffeurs?**

A: A dedicated driver and vehicle are assigned to you full-time for an agreed period. On-demand chauffeurs are booked per trip or per hour from AB's pool.

**Q2: Who employs the drivers on a dedicated contract?**

A: AB does. We handle vetting, payroll, relief cover and regulatory compliance under our local operating licences.

**Q3: Can routes change as our headcount changes?**

A: Yes. Routes are reviewed monthly, or sooner on request as staff numbers or office locations shift.

**Q4: Do you cover more than one country under one contract?**

A: Yes, through licensed AB entities in Saudi Arabia (KSA), the UAE and Qatar, all governed under a single master framework agreement.

**Q5: How are incidents handled?**

A: The Riyadh control room manages incidents live 24/7, dispatches standby relief vehicles immediately, and sends a written report to your nominated contact.
