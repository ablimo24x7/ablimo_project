Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed insights into our corporate operations, billing frameworks, and procurement standards.

How does the SAP Concur integration work?

Upon establishing a corporate account, your unique API key allows AB.Limo to automatically route e-receipts and expense data directly into your SAP Concur dashboard. We map charges to your designated cost-centers instantly, completely eliminating manual expense reporting for your executives.

What are your Duty of Care and vetting standards?

We exceed global enterprise standards. All AB.Limo chauffeurs undergo exhaustive background screenings, hold valid defensive driving certifications, and sign strict NDAs. Our vehicles are subject to rigorous preventative maintenance schedules and are tracked 24/7 via our central Intelligent Transport System (ITS).

Do you provide customized Long-Term Transportation Contracts?

Yes. We architect long-term lease structures inclusive of dedicated black fleets and vetted chauffeurs. These contracts are heavily utilized by government ministries, embassy protocol divisions, and mega-project workforce organizers requiring reliable, scalable mobility solutions over 1-5 year periods.

Can you support large-scale financial roadshows across multiple GCC cities?

Absolutely. Our As-Directed service and business roadshow logistics are designed specifically for complex, multi-stop itineraries. Our Transport Control Room handles dynamic routing, coordinating directly with your executive assistants to ensure seamless transitions between meetings in Riyadh, Dubai, Doha, and beyond.
