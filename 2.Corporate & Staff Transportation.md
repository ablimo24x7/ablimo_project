**H2 — Programmes we operate**

**H3 — Executive chauffeur programmes** On-call chauffeurs for leadership teams, with vehicle class, preferences and security requirements agreed per executive. Bookings come from executive assistants and are confirmed with chauffeur details before pickup.

**H3 — Dedicated vehicles and drivers** A chauffeur and vehicle assigned to one executive or department for months at a time. AB handles recruitment, vetting, payroll, relief cover and vehicle maintenance. A replacement vehicle arrives within \[Confirm: time\] if one is off the road.

**H3 — Staff shuttles and daily routes** Scheduled vans, minibuses or coaches between residential areas, offices and sites, with routes planned around shift times and reviewed as headcount changes. For large or remote workforces, see Workforce & Project-Site Transportation.

**H3 — Visitor and new-joiner programmes** Airport arrival, hotel, office and return for visiting executives, auditors, consultants and relocating staff, booked against your account.

**H3 — Roadshows and multi-city itineraries** Investor roadshows and site tours across Riyadh, Jeddah, the Eastern Province, Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Doha \[Confirm cities\], with one itinerary owner and the same standards in every city.

**H2 — How we run it**

1. **Requirements review.** Passenger groups, locations, hours, service levels and security needs.  
2. **Programme design.** Routes, vehicle classes, driver numbers, standby cover and booking rules, documented and signed off by you.  
3. **Mobilisation.** Vehicles prepared, drivers briefed on your sites and protocol, booking channels set up.  
4. **Daily operation.** Live tracking, dispatch changes and incident handling from the Riyadh control room, 24/7.  
5. **Monthly review.** Usage, punctuality and cost by department, with recommended changes.
