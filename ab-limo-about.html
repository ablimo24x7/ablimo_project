About AB

AB is a premium chauffeur and ground transportation partner, headquartered in Riyadh with offices in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Doha. We provide tailored mobility for businesses, government entities and diplomatic missions, and for the organizers of major sporting events, live entertainment, festivals, conferences and exhibitions across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar and the wider GCC.

Our history

A journey of continuous excellence

Since its founding in 2002, AB has grown into one of the region's leading providers of premium chauffeur and passenger transportation. Today it operates across Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Qatar, and has a growing presence in India.

2002

Foundation

Al Badiah Trading and Contracting Company was established in Saudi Arabia.

Saudi Arabia
2010

Entry into chauffeur transportation

The Al Badiah Group adds transportation to its activities, beginning with private chauffeur services.

Saudi Arabia
2020

An independent company

AB separates from Al Badiah Trading and becomes an independently registered company focused entirely on ground transportation.

Riyadh
2022

Expansion into Qatar

AB opens its office in Doha to serve the events sector and provide chauffeur services to corporate clients.

Doha
2022

Entry into the UAE

AB registers its office in Dubai and extends its services to the UAE travel and tourism sector.

Dubai
2024

A second UAE office

AB opens an office in Abu Dhabi, growing its presence and services across the UAE.

Abu Dhabi
2024

Global Passenger Network

AB joins the Global Passenger Network as its exclusive member from Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates.

Saudi Arabia & UAE
2026

Expansion into India

AB establishes its first company in Chennai, bringing its services to the Indian market. Chennai also serves as one of the group's support offices.

Chennai

01Licensed and fully compliant

We operate under the licensing and permit requirements of the transport authorities in each market we serve: TGA in Saudi Arabia, RTA in Dubai, ITC in Abu Dhabi and the Ministry of Transport in Qatar.

  • Every vehicle carries valid registration, insurance and operating permits
  • Every chauffeur holds the professional licenses and driver cards required locally
  • Renewals are tracked centrally, so nothing on the road is ever out of compliance

A modern, well-specified fleet. Vehicles stay in service only up to a set age and mileage limit, then they are replaced.

  • Multi-point inspection before every assignment: brakes, tires, lights and fluids; air conditioning and cabin comfort; seatbelts, first-aid kit and fire extinguisher; interior and exterior cleanliness
  • Planned maintenance on manufacturer schedules at authorised centres, every service recorded digitally
  • Presentation: cleaned and prepared before each trip, with bottled water, phone chargers and Wi-Fi on request
  • Technology on board: GPS tracking connected to our 24/7 control rooms

Every chauffeur passes background and security checks, document verification, a driving history review and a medical fitness check before joining AB, and holds the licenses required by TGA, RTA, ITC or MOT.

  • Defensive & safe driving
  • VIP & protocol etiquette
  • Customer service
  • Route & airport procedures
  • Emergency & incident response
  • Refresher training every 6 months and before every major event
  • Set uniform, grooming standards, code of conduct and strict confidentiality
  • Working-hour limits and mandatory rest periods, including on multi-day events
  • 24/7 control rooms in Riyadh, Dubai and Doha
  • Real-time monitoring of vehicle location, speed and trip status
  • Proactive alerts for delays, route changes and flight updates
  • Backup vehicles and chauffeurs on standby for every major operation

  • Audits: regular vehicle and chauffeur audits by our Quality Assurance team
  • Performance tracking: on-time performance, incident rates and client satisfaction measured across every operation
  • Client feedback: collected after assignments and reviewed by management
  • Incident management: clear reporting, investigation and corrective action
  • Continuous improvement: lessons from every major event feed back into our standards
