Executive Protection

Elite Vetting & TGA Compliance.

In the realm of enterprise mobility, the chauffeur is the ultimate failsafe. AB.Limo does not employ standard drivers; we deploy highly trained executive chauffeurs. We operate in absolute compliance with the Saudi Transport General Authority (TGA), enforcing a zero-tolerance policy for regulatory infractions.

Our proprietary vetting matrix includes exhaustive criminal background checks, motor vehicle record auditing, and mandatory defensive driving certifications. Furthermore, every chauffeur signs legally binding Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), ensuring that board-level conversations held within our vehicles remain strictly confidential.

For high-profile executives, we offer integrated Secure Executive Transportation. Our dispatch command interfaces directly with your corporate security teams, providing real-time GPS coordinates and route deviations to ensure your personnel are never unaccounted for in the capital.

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed insights into our operations at RUH Airport, KAFD, and Riyadh mega-events.

Do your chauffeurs have tarmac or VIP access at King Khalid International Airport (RUH)?

Yes, we coordinate directly with RUH FBOs (Fixed-Base Operators) and VIP terminals. For private aviation and diplomatic arrivals, our dispatch team ensures vetted chauffeurs are positioned appropriately for seamless Meet & Greet services upon landing.

Can you manage transport convoys for the Future Investment Initiative (FII) in Riyadh?

This is a core competency. During major summits like FII or LEAP, we activate our Transport Control Room. We coordinate VIP coach charters and executive sedans, utilizing live GPS tracking to navigate road closures and align with local security protocols.

Are your drivers cleared for entry into the Diplomatic Quarter (DQ) and KAFD?

Absolutely. All AB.Limo chauffeurs operating in Riyadh hold valid Saudi IDs and have undergone rigorous background checks. They are experienced in navigating the security checkpoints of both the DQ and the King Abdullah Financial District efficiently.

Can I book an As-Directed (Hourly) chauffeur for multiple meetings across Riyadh?

Yes, our As-Directed service is the preferred option for corporate roadshows. A dedicated chauffeur remains on standby at your location, providing ultimate flexibility for executives navigating unpredictable meeting schedules across the capital.
