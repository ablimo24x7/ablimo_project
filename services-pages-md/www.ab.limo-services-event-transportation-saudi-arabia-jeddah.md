## **Event transportation in Jeddah: your questions**

### **How early should we book event transportation in Jeddah?**

For large events, start as soon as dates and venues are fixed, ideally several weeks ahead, so we can hold vehicles and chauffeurs during busy seasons.

### **Can you run shuttles between hotels and venues?**

Yes. We design shuttle loops between partner hotels and venues, timed to session and show times, with extra vehicles at peaks.

### **Do you provide coordinators at JED and the venue?**

Yes. Coordinators run transport desks at the airport and the venue, and keep arrivals and departures on schedule.

### **Can AB handle VIP guests and talent?**

Yes. We run dedicated cars and convoys for principals, artists and protocol guests, with chauffeurs trained in discretion.

### **What if the programme changes on the day?**

Standby vehicles are positioned before the first pickup, and dispatchers can reroute shuttles or add cars within minutes.

### **Do you also offer chauffeurs outside event days?**

Yes. For everyday travel in the city, see our chauffeur service Jeddah page.
