## **How a Jeddah tour runs**

The driver collects guests from the hotel in the morning and starts in Al Balad before the heat of the day, followed by lunch and a drive along the Corniche. After a rest at the hotel, the evening ends at the King Fahd Fountain or a waterfront restaurant. A planner shares the timetable in advance, and our control room tracks the vehicle throughout.
