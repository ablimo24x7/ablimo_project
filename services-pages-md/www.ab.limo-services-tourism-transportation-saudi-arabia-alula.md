## **How an AlUla tour runs**

The driver collects guests early for the Hegra visit slot, returns to the resort for lunch and rest, then heads to the Old Town in the afternoon and Elephant Rock for sunset. A planner times each pickup to the booked visit slots, and our control room tracks the vehicle on the longer desert roads. AB also supported transport for the Azimuth music festival in AlUla.
