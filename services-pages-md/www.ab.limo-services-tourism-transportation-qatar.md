## **Frequently asked questions**

### **What is included in tourism transportation in Qatar?**

Every booking includes a licensed driver, a clean and air conditioned vehicle, fuel, the planned route and waiting time at each stop. AB plans the day around opening hours and traffic, and our Doha office tracks the vehicle throughout, so your guests only need to enjoy the visit.

### **Can I book a private tour driver in Qatar for a full day?**

Yes. We offer half day and full day bookings with a private tour driver Qatar guests can keep for the whole itinerary. The driver waits at each stop, helps with bags and adjusts the order of visits if your guests want to stay longer somewhere.

### **Which places do your tours in Qatar usually cover?**

Popular stops include Souq Waqif, the Museum of Islamic Art, the National Museum of Qatar, Katara Cultural Village, The Pearl, Lusail Boulevard and Al Wakrah Souq. We also run longer trips to Al Khor and the north coast for groups with more time.

### **Do you provide coaches for large tour groups in Qatar?**

Yes. We run minibuses and full size coaches for cruise passengers, incentive groups and school trips. A planner matches the vehicle to your group size and luggage, and can split large groups across several coaches with a shared timetable.

### **Can hotels and DMCs book tour transport in Qatar on account?**

Yes. Hotels and destination management companies can open an account with monthly invoicing and one contact for all bookings. We can also meet guests at Hamad International Airport (DOH) and continue with the tour on the same day.

### **How far in advance should I book?**

For small groups, a day or two is usually enough. For coaches, large groups or peak event periods, book as early as possible so we can hold vehicles and drivers for your dates.
